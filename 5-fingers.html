5 Fingers

Machine Learning + Market Profile for Nifty 50 intraday.

Nifty 50 intraday-க்கான Machine Learning + Market Profile கருவி.

The idea

As the market makes new highs and lows in the first minutes of the day, the model asks one question: is this likely to be the day's turning point? It answers with a probability, not a yes or no. A signal comes only when that probability crosses a level fixed in advance.

The model learned from ten years of Nifty 50 1-minute data (2016–2026). It was tested walk-forward — each year was predicted using only earlier years — and then on data it had never seen.

Fixed rules

  1. Market Profile filter. If Nifty opens outside the previous day's value area, there is no 5 Fingers trade that day.
  2. One trade a day. At most one 5 Fingers trade per day — LONG from a low, SHORT from a high.
  3. Stop loss fixed in advance, and moved to the entry price (breakeven) once the trade is 30 points in profit.
  4. Time exit at 14:50. No discretion, no averaging, no re-entry.

Honest limits

Machine learning gives a probability, not a promise. Many signals will lose. Some weeks and months will end in a loss. If market behaviour changes, the model can weaken — that is why every result is published and monitored. Tools marked experimental (PDH/PDL, ORB 30, Europe risk-off) are still being evaluated live.
